A. Business Context

The company sells FMCG products across multiple categories, brands and SKUs, distributed through General Trade ,Modern Trade & E commerce channels across several regions and states. Sales volumes have grown, but management has noticed that revenue growth is not consistently translating into proportional gross-profit growth, and discounting activity has increased without a clear read on its return. Category, channel and regional teams currently report performance in isolation, so leadership lacks one consolidated view of where sales are genuinely profitable versus where they are being sustained by margin-eroding discounts. This analysis is commissioned to give the commercial leadership team a single, decision-ready view ahead of the quarterly business review.

B. Business Problem

Management lacks visibility into which regions, channels, categories, brands and SKUs are driving profitable sales growth, and whether increased discounting is generating sufficient volume to justify the associated margin erosion. This makes it difficult to prioritize commercial investment and address margin dilution before it compounds.

C. Business Objective

To analyze Net Sales, Units, Gross Profit and Gross Margin % across region, channel, category, brand and time, and to assess whether discounting and promotional activity is generating sufficient volume to justify margin erosion, in order to identify profitable growth opportunities and prioritize two or three commercial levers for the next quarter.


D. Analysis Questions

Q1. Which regions deliver the strongest combination of Net Sales, Units and Gross Profit, and how does Gross Margin % vary across them?
Q2. Which sales channels generate the highest revenue, and do any channels show high sales but comparatively weak Gross Margin %?	
Q3. Which product categories contribute the most to sales and profit, and does the highest-selling category also carry the strongest margin?
Q4. Which brands show a gap between their share of sales and their share of gross profit?
Q5. Which SKUs generate high sales but comparatively low Gross Margin %, indicating profit-leakage candidates?
Q6. How do Net Sales, Units and Gross Margin % vary across discount bands — are higher discount levels associated with lower margins without a proportional increase in volume?	
Q7. Across Brands, Regions and Channels, where did Net Sales growth outpace Gross Profit growth from 2024 to 2025, indicating potential margin dilution?
Q8. Does promotional discounting generate proportionally higher volume relative to its impact on Gross Margin?
