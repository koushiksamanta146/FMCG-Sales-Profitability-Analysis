# FMCG Sales & Profitability Analysis

**Portfolio Project | Excel | Business Analytics | Commercial Performance Analysis**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes FMCG sales and profitability performance across regions, channels, categories, brands and SKUs.

The objective is to understand not only where sales are being generated, but also where profitability is strong, where margins are under pressure, and which commercial areas require management attention.

The project was developed as an **industry-style commercial analysis and QBR portfolio project** using Excel.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Revenue growth is not always translating into proportional gross-profit growth, while discounting and promotional activity require closer evaluation.

Management needs a consolidated view of:

* Which regions and channels are driving profitable sales
* Which categories and brands contribute most to revenue and profit
* Which SKUs may have potential margin leakage
* How discount levels relate to gross margin and transaction volume
* Where sales growth and gross-profit growth are not aligned
* Whether promotional activity generates meaningful volume response

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

To analyze **Net Sales, Units, Gross Profit and Gross Margin %** across key business dimensions, evaluate discounting and promotional performance, identify potential profitability risks, and highlight commercial levers for the next quarter.

---

## 📊 Key Business Questions

The project addresses eight core business questions:

1. Which regions deliver the strongest combination of Net Sales, Units and Gross Profit?
2. Which sales channels generate the highest revenue, and how does their Gross Margin compare?
3. Which product categories contribute most to sales and profit?
4. Which brands show a gap between their share of sales and share of gross profit?
5. Which SKUs generate high sales but comparatively low Gross Margin %?
6. How does Gross Margin vary across different discount bands?
7. Where is Net Sales growth outpacing Gross Profit growth?
8. Does promotional activity generate proportionally higher transaction volume?

---

## 📈 Key Findings

### Overall Performance

* **Net Sales:** 329.4M
* **Gross Profit:** 104.6M
* **Gross Margin:** 31.7%
* **Total Units Sold:** 4.06M

### Commercial Findings

* **North** recorded the highest reported regional Net Sales at **85.1M**, with a Gross Margin of **37.1%**.
* **E-Commerce** generated the highest channel Net Sales at **111.7M**, but had a comparatively lower Gross Margin of **26.7%**.
* **Beverages** led category sales at **87.0M**, while **Personal Care** recorded a substantially higher Gross Margin of **41.7%**.
* **74 of 180 SKUs** were flagged as potential profit-leakage candidates based on the project's margin criteria.
* Gross Margin declined from **34.1%** in the 0–5% discount band to **16.4%** in the >20% discount band, while average units per transaction remained broadly similar.
* Across selected brands, cases were identified where **Sales Growth exceeded Gross Profit Growth**, indicating areas requiring closer margin monitoring.

> **Note:** Discount-band findings indicate an observed association in the dataset and should not be interpreted as proof of causation.

---

## 💡 Commercial Levers

Based on the analysis, three areas were identified for management attention:

1. **Discount Governance**
   Strengthen discount guardrails where higher discount depth is not associated with a proportional volume response.

2. **E-Commerce Economics**
   Review pricing, trade terms and promotional economics in the highest-sales channel.

3. **SKU-Level Margin Management**
   Review flagged low-margin SKUs for potential pricing, cost and commercial-term opportunities.
